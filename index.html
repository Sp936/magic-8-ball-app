Magic Dopamine Ball

ℹ️ How to use

1. Add your options
Anything you could realistically do right now.
Small tasks = faster wins. Assign energy for each task before pressing 'add task' (see below).

2. Assign energy for tasks
💤 Low → easy
⚡ Medium → moderate
🔥 High → harder

3. Match your current energy
Just above the 'Decide' button is a separate 'Your energy right now' selector — that's the one to update before pressing Decide. Be honest. This is what makes it work.

4. Press Decide
No overthinking. Let it choose.

5. Act immediately
The magic is in moving before hesitation.

6. Tap “I did it”
Lock in the reward. Build your streak 🔥

🧠 Why this works
Completing small tasks releases dopamine.
That dopamine makes starting the next task easier.
Momentum beats motivation — every time.

✨ Rule:
Don’t question the choice. Just count down from 3 and move.

💾 Save tasks:
Tasks will autodelete once they have been completed (once they've been selected by the dopamine ball and you have then clicked 'I did it'), unless the 'Save tasks' feature is toggled on. Open the slide-up drawer at the bottom of the screen to view all tasks and manage them. Pressing the ✕ next to a task manually deletes it.

↻ Weekly repeats:
Tick 'Repeat weekly' when adding a task and pick a day — that task will automatically reappear on the same day every week, whether or not 'Save tasks' is switched on. Manage or remove weekly repeats from the slide-up drawer.
